Chilaquiles

Description

Chilaquiles are a quintessential Mexican breakfast dish designed to breathe life into leftover corn tortillas. Crisp, fried tortilla triangles are lightly simmered in a vibrant, savory red tomato and chili sauce until they reach a perfect balance of tender yet structurally sound. Typically crowned with shredded chicken, a drizzle of Mexican crema, crumbled cotija cheese, and raw onion rings, they offer a comforting combination of smoky spice, rich corn flavor, and cool, tangy toppings.

Ingredients

  • 12 stale corn tortillas (cut into triangles)
  • 4 large Roma tomatoes
  • 2 dried guajillo chilies (stems and seeds removed)
  • 1 jalapeño or serrano pepper
  • 1/2 medium white onion (divided: half for the sauce, half sliced for garnish)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 cup Mexican crema or sour cream
  • 1/2 cup crumbled cotija or queso fresco
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken (optional)

Steps

  1. Fry the Tortillas: Heat about 1/2 inch of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the tortilla triangles in batches until crisp and golden brown. Drain on paper towels and sprinkle lightly with salt.
  2. Make the Sauce Base: In a medium pot, boil the tomatoes, guajillo chilies, jalapeño, half of the onion, and garlic in water for about 10 minutes until the tomatoes are soft and the dried chilies are pliable.
  3. Blend: Transfer the boiled ingredients to a blender with 1/2 cup of the cooking water and a teaspoon of salt. Blend until completely smooth.
  4. Cook the Sauce: Pour a tablespoon of oil into a deep pan or skillet over medium heat. Pour in the blended sauce (strain it through a mesh sieve if you want it extra smooth). Bring to a simmer and let it cook for 5 minutes to deepen the flavors.
  5. Assemble: Stir the fried tortilla chips (and shredded chicken, if using) gently into the hot sauce. Turn off the heat immediately—you want the chips coated and slightly softened, but not soggy. Top with crema, crumbled cheese, and sliced onions before serving.
