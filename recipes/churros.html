Churros

Description

Churros are a beloved sweet snack made from a simple choux-like pastry dough that is piped through a star-shaped nozzle into hot oil. This specific star shape isn't just for looks; the ridges create more surface area for maximum crispiness and prevent the steam inside from building up and bursting. Fried until deep golden brown, these fluted pastry sticks are immediately rolled in a generous mix of granulated sugar and ground cinnamon, resulting in an airy, tender interior surrounded by a shatteringly crisp, sweet shell.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 2 1/2 tbsp granulated sugar (plus 1/2 cup separate for coating)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil (plus more for deep frying)
  • 1 cup all-purpose flou
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon

Steps

  1. Boil the Liquid: In a small saucepan over medium-high heat, combine the water, 2 1/2 tablespoons of sugar, salt, and 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil, then remove it from the heat.
  2. Mix the Dough: Stir in the flour all at once using a wooden spoon. Mix vigorously until the dough forms a smooth, cohesive ball that pulls away from the sides of the pan. Let it cool for 5 minutes
  3. Prep the Piping Bag: Transfer the dough into a sturdy pastry bag fitted with a large closed-star tip (like an Open Star 1M).
  4. Fry: Heat 2 inches of oil in a heavy-bottomed pot to 375°F (190°C). Pipe 4-to-6-inch strips of dough directly into the hot oil, cutting the ends cleanly with clean kitchen shears. Fry for about 2 minutes per side until they are an even golden brown.
  5. Coat: Mix the remaining 1/2 cup of sugar and the ground cinnamon in a shallow dish. Remove the churros from the oil, drain briefly on paper towels for just a few seconds, and roll them immediately in the cinnamon-sugar mix while hot so it sticks perfectly.
