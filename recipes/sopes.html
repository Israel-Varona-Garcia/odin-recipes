Sopes

Description

Sopes are thick, savory corn cakes made from masa (corn dough) that feature a unique, hand-pinched rim designed to hold an array of delicious toppings. The dough is first pressed into thick rounds, cooked briefly on a dry griddle (comal) to seal the shape, and then the edges are pinched upwards while still hot to form a shallow boat. Lightly fried just before serving to add a delicate exterior crunch, they are traditionally layered with warm refried beans, shredded meats, crumbled cheese, lettuce, and salsa for a perfect handheld meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups masa harina (instant corn masa flour)
  • 1 1/4 cups warm water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 1/2 cups warm refried beans
  • 1 cup crumbled queso fresco or cotija
  • Toppings of choice: shredded lettuce, salsa, sliced radishes, or cooked shredded meat

Steps

  1. Make the Masa: In a large bowl, mix the masa harina and salt. Gradually pour in the warm water, kneading with your hands until a soft, smooth dough forms. It should feel like Play-Doh—if it cracks easily, add a splash more water; if it sticks to your hands, add a dusting of masa harina
  2. Shape the Rounds: Divide the dough into 8 equal balls. Using a tortilla press lined with plastic, or your hands, gently flatten each ball into a thick disc about 1/4-inch thick and 3 to 4 inches wide.
  3. Griddle Cook: Heat a dry skillet or griddle over medium-high heat. Cook each disc for about 1 to 2 minutes per side until lightly flipped, cooked through, and dotted with faint brown spots.
  4. Pinch the Borders: Remove the warm discs from the heat. Using a clean kitchen towel to protect your fingers from the heat, use your thumb and forefinger to pinch the outer edges of the disc upward, creating a raised rim all the way around the border.
  5. Flash Fry and Fill: Heat a few tablespoons of oil in a skillet over medium heat. Fry the sopes for about 1 minute on each side until the bottom is lightly golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels, then immediately fill the center with warm refried beans and your favorite toppings.
